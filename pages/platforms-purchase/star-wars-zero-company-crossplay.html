Star Wars Zero Company Crossplay & Platforms: Three Platforms, One Single-Player Truth

Category: Platforms & Purchase • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: Star Wars Zero Company launched August 27, 2026 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC simultaneously — and crossplay doesn't apply because the game is single-player turn-based tactics. There are no lobbies to bridge. What platform shoppers need: identical launch everywhere, Steam Achievements and Trading Cards on PC, and no announced cross-save or cross-progression structure between ecosystems.

The Direct Answer: A Single-Player Tactics Game Has No Crossplay

Crossplay requires multiplayer pools; Steam's category set lists Single-player with no online modes — this is a personal campaign about your company, your permadeath settings and your squad relationships. So every 'is there crossplay' query ends structurally: nothing to cross-play with. What the three-platform launch DOES make relevant is the ecosystem question: where should you buy, and does progress follow you.

Platform Matrix: Simultaneous Launch, Divergent Ecosystems

PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC launched the same day (August 27, 2026) per EA's launch announcement — no stagger, no exclusivity window. PC carries the Steam ecosystem specifics: achievements, Steam Trading Cards (a category most AAA skip), Steam Cloud, HDR support, and Family Sharing. Deluxe pricing runs ~$10 higher on console ($69.99 vs $59.99) per launch-window reporting. Cross-save/cross-progression between platforms: not announced. For a save-anytime single-player campaign, assume one ecosystem = one campaign unless EA says otherwise.

Platform Pick Guidance

PC tacticians: the mouse-driven cover-targeting genre standard applies; the spec floor (GTX 1080/16 GB) is generous for what the game needs at 1080p — see our requirements page. Console players: DualShock/DualSense support is formally listed, and the turn-based design means no framerate-sensitive disadvantage between platforms. Households with mixed platforms: parallel campaigns, no shared saves — plan purchases per ecosystem. The editions breakdown covers where the Deluxe cosmetics land per platform.
