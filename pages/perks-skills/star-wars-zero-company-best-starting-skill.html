Star Wars Zero Company Best Starting Class: The Week-One Meta, Sourced

Category: Classes & Perks • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: The class system holds 12 specializations — 8 standard (Assault, Heavy, Sharpshooter, Scoundrel among them) and 4 exotic — and the week-one community consensus is unusually united: the Scoundrel is the strongest starting pick, while exotic unit Cly Kullervo sits at the top of the unit tier discussion. Both claims come from the fastest-growing wiki layer in the game's ecosystem; both carry a 'provisional meta' label until balance patches say otherwise.

The 12 Specializations, Structurally

Official marketing confirms 12 total specializations: 8 standard classes and 4 exotic classes. Named standard classes from official-and-launch material: Assault, Heavy, Sharpshooter, Scoundrel. The exotic four are the hero-style outliers — unique kits rather than archetype fills. Full class breakdowns are the fastest-growing section of the Fextralife wiki (79 pages within two days of launch, including a loadout planner), which is where the competitive conversation lives right now.

The Week-One Consensus: Scoundrel First

The launch-window community answer to 'best starting class' is the Scoundrel — the consensus forms around its early-game economy of aggressive repositioning and its scaling into the mid-campaign. On units, the exotic Cly Kullervo anchors most early tier lists. Two honesty labels on this: first, week-one metas in tactics games are provisional by nature (XCOM-history balance swings within the first month are the norm); second, 'best' here means 'best expected value for a first campaign' — class enjoyment still outruns tier lists for a single-player, relationship-driven campaign.

Roster Construction Over Class Chasing

The guides' shared tactical advice — Into Indie Games and Hack the Minotaur alike — is that coverage overlaps beat individual class power: a roster with a heavy front, a sharpshooter backline, a scoundrel flex and a support/tech slot absorbs bad missions better than four optimized copies of the meta pick. With permadeath enabled, the roster IS the save file — depth of twelve matters more than the top one.

How This Page Tracks the Meta
