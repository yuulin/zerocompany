Star Wars Zero Company Editions Guide: Standard vs Deluxe vs Collector's & Preorder Bonus

Category: Guides & Walkthroughs • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: Three ways to buy: Standard ($49.99, base game), Deluxe (~$59.99 PC / $69.99 console — cosmetic packs including uniforms, helmets, armor sets, tattoos, painted weapon themes and a Clone Battalion set, all live at first launch), and a physical Collector's Edition on all three platforms. The preorder bonus was the Crystalline Astromech cosmetic pack. Everything beyond Standard is cosmetics — no gameplay power anywhere.

Deluxe Math: Who Should Pay the Extra $10

The Deluxe tier is a uniform and identity pack for the company you'll spend dozens of hours with — worth it exactly to the degree that customizing your squad's look matters to you. Star Wars fans who already know they'll dress the squad in Clone Battalion colors: day-one Deluxe is the honest buy. Value-first players lose literally nothing mechanical on Standard — every specialization, mission and ending is identical. Collector's Edition hunters should compare physical configurations across retailers before committing, as contents vary by region.

Preorder Bonus Status

The Crystalline Astromech cosmetic pack attached to preorders. Post-launch, preorder windows typically close — whether the pack returns later (as many publishers' cosmetic packs eventually do) is unannounced; this page notes it the day anything official appears. Everything else about the editions is stable and listed above.

Frequently Asked Directives

What's in the Star Wars Zero Company Deluxe Edition? ▼
Cosmetic packs at first launch: uniforms, helmets, armor sets, tattoos, painted weapon themes and a Clone Battalion set — plus the base game. Roughly $10 over Standard.
Is the Deluxe Edition worth it? ▼
Only if squad customization matters to you — all Deluxe content is cosmetic, and Standard contains the complete game with all 12 specializations.
What was the preorder bonus for Star Wars Zero Company? ▼
The Crystalline Astromech cosmetic pack.
Is there a Collector's Edition? ▼
Yes, physical, available for PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC — contents vary by retailer.
Does any edition include gameplay advantages? ▼
No — every edition's extra content is cosmetic, which for a single-player tactics campaign means identical mechanics across all versions.
