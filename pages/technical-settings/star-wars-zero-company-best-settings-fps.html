Star Wars Zero Company Settings & Performance Guide: Turn-Based Changes the Rules

Category: Technical & Optimization • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: Star Wars Zero Company ships with the most tactics-friendly feature set in recent memory: Save Anytime, Playable without Timed Input, and Adjustable Difficulty are formal Steam categories — meaning fps dips and low-end hardware cost you presentation, never tactics. The settings strategy therefore inverts the shooter playbook: prioritize visual clarity of the battlefield over framerate paranoia.

The Feature Set That Changes Everything

Steam's official listing for the game includes Save Anytime, Playable without Timed Input, Adjustable Difficulty, and HDR support. Read together: you can save mid-mission, there is no twitch reflex gate anywhere in the design, and difficulty scales separately from your aim. For settings, this means stutter during a cinematic is cosmetic — reload a save and it replays fine. The spec sheet (GTX 1080 floor, RTX 3080 recommended for 1440p60) exists to serve the presentation layer, not to gate tactics.

Settings Priorities for Tactics Players

  • Clarity over effects: cover indicators, line-of-sight cones, and elevation shading are the real UI — if any post-processing muddies them at your resolution, dial effects down before resolution.
  • HDR on if you have it — it's a formal category, and dark-space missions benefit most.
  • Sub-60 fps is playable — with no timed input, 40 fps on a midrig loses nothing but camera smoothness in kill-cams.
  • Partial controller support is listed — mouse-and-keyboard remains the precision default for cover-targeting.

Known Rough Edges at Launch

Windows Central's review — otherwise glowing — notes "some glitches here and there", and Shacknews' more mixed take says the game "holds its punches." Two days post-launch, the community hub is the live channel for launch-week bugs and their workarounds; patch tracking lives on our release and patch page.

Frequently Asked Directives

Does Star Wars Zero Company need high fps? ▼
No — Steam lists Playable without Timed Input as a formal category. Turn-based play is identical at 40 or 120 fps; only kill-cam smoothness changes.
Does it support HDR? ▼
Yes — HDR is an official Steam category for the title.
Can I save mid-mission? ▼
Yes — Save Anytime is a listed feature, rare for the tactics genre.
Are there difficulty options? ▼
Adjustable Difficulty is an official category, scaling separately from input skill.
