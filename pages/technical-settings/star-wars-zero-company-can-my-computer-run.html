Star Wars Zero Company System Requirements: Official PC Specs (1080p vs 1440p60)

Category: Technical & Optimization • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: Official specs: minimum is an i5-8400 / Ryzen 5 2600X with 16 GB RAM and a GTX 1080 for 1080p; the recommended tier is genuinely heavy — i7-10700K, 32 GB RAM, RTX 3080 for 1440p at 60 fps. 50 GB install, DirectX 12, and Steam flags it Verified-adjacent with partial controller support. For a turn-based tactics game, that recommended tier is a statement about how good it looks, not what the gameplay demands.

Official Requirements (Steam, Verified Aug 29)

Pulled from the Steam page for AppID 2075800:

  • Minimum (1080p): Windows 10/11 64-bit · Core i5-8400 or Ryzen 5 2600X · 16 GB RAM · GTX 1080 / RX 5600 XT / Arc B580 · DX12 · 50 GB storage
  • Recommended (1440p @ 60): Windows 11 · Core i7-10700K or Ryzen 7 3700X · 32 GB RAM · RTX 3080 / RX 7800 XT · 50 GB

Two lines stand out: 16 GB is the entry fee (8 GB machines are out), and the recommended GPU tier (RTX 3080-class) would be high for a twitch shooter, let alone a turn-based tactics title — Unreal Engine 5 cinematic presentation is where that budget goes.

What a Turn-Based Game Does With All That GPU

The answer is in the review consensus: Windows Central's review describes turn-based tactics meeting third-person cinematography — kill-cam presentation, authored camera work, andClone Wars-era spectacle between commands. The framerate target on the recommended row (1440p/60) is a cinematography budget, not a gameplay requirement: the Steam build also ships Playable without Timed Input and Save Anytime as formal categories, so a player on minimum specs loses nothing mechanically.

Practical Guidance

A GTX 1080 / 5600 XT machine plays the full campaign at 1080p with no tactical disadvantage — pause-anytime play makes framerate far more forgiving than in action titles. The 50 GB install wants an SSD per usual UE5 streaming behavior. Console players (PS5, Xbox Series X|S) skip this matrix entirely — all three platforms launched together on August 27.
