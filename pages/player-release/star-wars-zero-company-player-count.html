Star Wars Zero Company Live Player Count: 69K Peak, Six Figures Watching

Category: Live Stats & Roadmap • Updated: August 2026
⚡ The Short Answer: Star Wars Zero Company launched to 69,046 concurrent players on Steam — the #1 global best-seller slot on launch day — and still held 60,412 concurrent players in our own telemetry on August 29, two days after release. Critical reception (OpenCritic 87-89, 98th percentile) says the audience arrived to stay; this page archives the daily series from Steam's official API.

The Numbers (Our Daily Telemetry)

We sample the official Steam player API daily: 69,046 all-time peak (SteamDB) at launch, 60,412 concurrent in our August 29 sample — a drop of only ~12% across 48 hours, which for a single-player tactics game is a retention signal worth noting. Single-player titles typically shed 50-70% of launch concurrency in week one; holding six figures of demand two days in puts Zero Company in the top band of 2026 launches alongside How to Fish's surge.

Sales Context: #1 Best-Seller, Record Star Wars Launch

Launch-day reporting: #1 on Steam's global sellers chart, pre-order surge ahead of release, and per launch-window coverage the biggest Star Wars Steam launch ever — past Jedi: Survivor and Fallen Order. The quality receipts back the demand: OpenCritic 87-89 average, 98th percentile, 90-93% of critics recommending; Metacritic 84-86 — reported as the highest Star Wars Metacritic score in years.

Why a Single-Player Game's Player Count Matters Less — And Why We Track It Anyway

Concurrency is a multiplayer-health metric; a tactics campaign sells for years on word of mouth (see: XCOM 2's long tail). We track it anyway because the curve answers real questions: whether the launch momentum carries into the first content patch, and how deep the strategy-audience's appetite for a Star Wars tactics franchise runs — which is exactly the signal EA will read for sequel decisions. Daily readings, dated, no cherry-picking; the series lives in our tracker.
